## The diagnosis it acts on

From `substrates.py`:

```python
elif action == SLOW:
    self.slow.write(key, value, repeats=self.cfg.slow_consolidation_steps)
```

**In EXP-001, choosing `SLOW` is committing.** Durable state is written directly
by the allocator and no FAST→SLOW promotion exists anywhere in that codebase. So
no EXP-001 arm can hold commitment fixed while varying allocation — the
experiment is the wrong shape for CCS-C4, not merely underpowered for it.

## The structural change

Remove `SLOW` from the allocation action space; reach durability only through a
separate commitment operator that promotes pending FAST content on a fixed
schedule, with **no selection, no ranking and no evidence gate**.

Allocation then decides *what becomes a candidate*; commitment decides *whether
and when candidates become durable*.

## Factorial and the criterion CCS-C4 needs

3 × 2, paired seeds. Allocation: `LEARNED` / `HEURISTIC_EXT` /
`ACTION_MATCHED_RANDOM`. Commitment: `IMMEDIATE` / `CADENCE_k`.

`ACTION_MATCHED_RANDOM` is the load-bearing arm: it matches the learned arm's
realized action counts, hence its FAST count, hence its realized commit count.
`LEARNED − ACTION_MATCHED_RANDOM` therefore isolates *which item was placed
where* with commitment volume held fixed.

> **F-C4** — if that contrast's CI includes zero in **either** commitment regime,
> after realized commit counts and budgets are matched, the apparent allocation
> advantage is fully explained by commitment timing or count. CCS-C4 is not
> settled and EXP-001's partial support does not extend.

Recorded as CCS-C4's third falsifier. **F-SIGN** — opposite signs across regimes
— is recorded as a new falsifier of **CCS-C1**, and is the more interesting
outcome: it would mean allocation's optimum depends on the commitment regime,
contradicting separability directly.

## Two limitations recorded before execution

**Scope.** Commitment is a *schedule*, not an evidence gate, because importing an
unvalidated State Promotion mechanism is forbidden. A successful EXP-003 would
therefore settle separability from commitment **timing** only. CCS-C4 does not
restrict the commit family, so this claim may need **two** settlement steps, and
even a clean EXP-003 result would itself be partial-scope evidence.

**Identifiability.** The factors are not fully orthogonal: allocation determines
the *supply* of commit candidates. Handled by construction for the primary
contrast, only partly for the interaction term, where arms differ in commit
supply as well as allocation content. Written into the design rather than
discovered afterwards.
