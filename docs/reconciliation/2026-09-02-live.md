### `modular-consolidation` — CCS-C6, and a new CCS-C11

The most consequential finding of this reconciliation.

That track's development simulator (closed-form ridge learner, synthetic CAMS
stream with known ground-truth skill count) establishes that **retention versus
capacity is monotone non-decreasing under any competent routing**, replicated
across three independent mechanisms. A module that is never selected cannot
damage a prediction. Its EXP-000 found a fixed bank of the lifecycle's own final
size beating the full lifecycle by 7.8 points at identical capacity.

The consequence that track draws: in the unbounded regime at matched capacity,
consolidation has nothing to fix, and its only available benefit is compression
rather than reduced forgetting. Its track-local ledger marks the corresponding
claim **falsified**.

**The umbrella did not inherit that status.** Two reasons, both binding:

1. It is a development simulator on a closed-form learner, explicitly not
   evidence about real models.
2. That track's own contamination rule states its results are not evidence for
   any claim in another track.

Marking CCS-C6 `falsified` here would violate both. It is held at `unresolved`
with the two against-direction entries attached and a note saying it should be
expected to end at `falsified`.

**CCS-C6 was not rewritten to survive.** The unbounded-regime statement is kept
as-is. The live constrained question is recorded as a **new claim, CCS-C11**,
covering the binding-ceiling regime where merge, evict and deny genuinely differ:

| freeing a slot by | retention | plasticity | forgetting |
|---|---|---|---|
| refusing to spawn (`B-DENY`) | **0.762** | 0.775 | **0.032** |
| pooling (`B-MERGE`) | 0.745 | 0.798 | 0.071 |
| deleting (`B-EVICT-LRU`) | 0.541 | **0.854** | 0.324 |

merge − evict = **+0.204** retention, CI [+0.125, +0.296]. merge − deny = −0.017
with the CI spanning zero, concealing plasticity +0.023 and forgetting +0.039
*both* excluding zero — a two-way trade that a single retention headline would
misreport. merge − random-merge = +0.009, CI spanning zero, so the pair-selection
criterion did not survive to the aggregate.

Adding a claim rather than editing one is the difference between narrowing a
question and moving a goalpost. The record now shows both.

## Terminology divergence — recorded, not resolved

`plasticity-routing` reads **COMMIT** as an outward-facing commitment (external
action, tool call, something irreversible in the world) and states that its
durable `SLOW` write is "not a commitment in the CCS sense". The umbrella defines
COMMIT as the decision that a change becomes durable, which includes an internal
durable write.

Both readings are coherent; they are not the same operator. Under the narrower
reading, `plasticity-routing`'s `SLOW` action is an allocation decision that the
umbrella would class as a commitment — which is precisely the separability
question CCS-C4 exists to settle.

The umbrella records the disagreement rather than harmonising the vocabulary.
Harmonising it now would settle by definition a question meant to be settled by
experiment. Logged in [TERMINOLOGY.md](../TERMINOLOGY.md#outstanding-divergence--commit)
and as an F4 watch item in [FAILURE-CRITERIA.md](../FAILURE-CRITERIA.md).

## Admissibility rules adopted programme-wide

Five controls from the newer tracks were strong enough to generalise, and are now
inherited by any new sibling: capacity as a first-class metric including cold
storage and routing compute; matching a dynamic method against a fixed
configuration of its own realised size; a prohibition on weakening the primary
comparator; ground-truth fields for scoring only; and locking the
corruption/difficulty process with a hash in every manifest. See
[EVIDENCE-MAP.md](../EVIDENCE-MAP.md#additions-adopted-at-the-2026-09-02-reconciliation).

## An obstacle that did not exist at the first survey

The four tracks do not share a budget-accounting scheme. `state-promotion` counts
write units; `plasticity-routing` uses a unified cost table with one write
ceiling; `modular-consolidation` counts capacity, cold storage and total
algorithmic FLOPs; `lifetime-integrity` counts evidence reads against a capped
log. These are not interchangeable, and integration gate I2 requires budgets to be
addable before any integrated total means anything.

Reconciling them is real work that nobody currently owns, and it is a prerequisite
for `EXP-I1` rather than a detail of it. Recorded in
[INTEGRATION-CRITERIA.md](../INTEGRATION-CRITERIA.md#current-status--reconciled-2026-09-02).
